### 👩‍💻 About Me

I get more excited by *"can this even be done?"* than by *"is this the safe choice?"* I actively push new, unfamiliar technologies into real projects instead of sticking to what I already know — even when that means slower, messier progress instead of a tidy row of finished apps.

That's on purpose. Most of my repos look like works-in-progress because they *are* — they're where I test ideas, hit walls, and learn by actually building instead of just reading docs. I'd rather have ten ambitious experiments than one safe, polished clone.

- 🧪 Integrate new tech into real projects on purpose, not just tutorials — comfort zone is the thing I'm avoiding
- 🚧 Most repos are active experiments, not finished showcases — progress over polish
- 🔁 Treat mistakes as the fastest way to learn, not something to hide or avoid
- 🌐 Versatile by choice — broad and adaptable beats narrow and "done"
- 🧩 Flagship lab: **Lum & Auro** — TypeScript monorepo blog (JWT, shared Zod, TanStack Query, design tokens + dark mode)

### 🌿 Lum & Auro — Blog Platform
*`Flagship lab · V3 rewrite`*
<div align="center">
  <a href="https://www.elifyuzer.dev">
    <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/elif-yuzer/bitirme-revize/main/apps/web/public/blogapp.gif" width="720" alt="Lum & Auro blog platform demo"/>

Full-stack editorial blog rebuilt from a JS-only client into a **TypeScript monorepo** — JWT auth, shared Zod contracts, TanStack Query, design tokens + dark mode, Tiptap editor. Deployed on Vercel + Render.
